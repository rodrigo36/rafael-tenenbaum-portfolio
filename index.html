01 The Maker
Est. craft

Where botany, chemistry and memory meet in a glass.

Rafael Tenenbaum Szajubok treats the bar as a living laboratory. Each build begins long before the shake — foraging aromatics, fermenting, infusing and layering textures until a drink becomes its own small world.

His approach is quiet and precise: seasonal ingredients, house ferments and a deep respect for balance. The result is cocktails that feel inevitable — complex, yet effortless to fall into.
